# Demo video script, PesaAI (target 2:30, hard cap 3:00)

Record at 1080p. Show the real app. Judges stop at 3 minutes, so front-load the
PayPal + AI + agent proof. Upload to YouTube, public.

## Shot list

**0:00-0:20 Hook + problem**
On camera or voiceover over the dashboard:
"Millions of Kenyans earn in US dollars through PayPal, then spend locally. Fees
pile up, income is lumpy, and good money advice is scarce. PesaAI is an AI money
coach that sits on top of PayPal and can actually move money for you."

**0:20-0:40 Dashboard tour**
Show the ledger (AG Grid): income, spending, net, the pinned total. Point out
real PayPal rows vs the `sim` M-Pesa sample rows. Note the live status chips:
"PayPal sandbox live, AI gpt-4o-mini."

**0:40-1:10 AI coach (the AI proof)**
Point out the proactive suggestion the coach already made on load ("it reviewed
my ledger and suggested moving money to savings, before I asked anything").
Click Insights, read the tailored result aloud. Then ask in chat: "How do I cut
my transaction fees?" Toggle SW and ask one question in Swahili to show
bilingual support. Optionally tap the microphone and speak the question, to show
voice input (test it first; skip on camera if the browser mic is unreliable).

**1:10-2:05 Agentic payment (the differentiator)**
Type: "send $15 to jane@example.com for the logo design."
Narrate: "I didn't fill a form. The AI read that, decided it's a payment, and
proposed it." Show the confirmation card. Say: "Nothing moves until I confirm,
and payments are capped for safety." Click Confirm & send. Show the payout
success message and the new row appearing in the ledger. This is agentic
commerce: a conversation that ends in a real PayPal payout.

**2:05-2:25 PayPal top-up (close the PayPal loop)**
Enter an amount, click Pay with PayPal, show the real sandbox checkout, approve,
Complete top-up, and the balance update.

**2:25-2:40 Close**
"PesaAI: PayPal plus AI, with a human in the loop. Real sandbox payments, real
AI, built for Kenyan freelancers. Thanks for watching."

## Tips
- Wake the backend before recording (hit the app once) so there is no cold-start
  pause on camera.
- Do a dry run of the agent line; keep the recipient and amount simple.
- Keep each section tight; if you run long, cut the Swahili clip, not the agent
  demo.
- No copyrighted music. Use silence or a royalty-free track.
